Contents, back to front: (1) a radial gradient in `defs` from light cyan #7DF9FF at about 55 percent opacity at the center to fully transparent, used by a halo circle centered at 32,32 with radius 14; (2) the lattice: three horizontal lines at y 8, 32 and 56 spanning x 8 to 56, and three vertical lines at x 8, 32 and 56 spanning y 8 to 56, stroke #00E5FF, stroke-width 2, opacity about 0.35; (3) the rising data line as a polyline through lattice nodes only, points 8,56 32,32 56,8, stroke #7DF9FF, stroke-width 2, round caps and joins, no fill; (4) the eight outer lattice nodes as circles of radius 3 filled #00E5FF; (5) the bright center node at 32,32, radius 7, fill #00E5FF.
